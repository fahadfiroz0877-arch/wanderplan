About WanderPlan

India's Travel Community,
Built from the Road Up.

WanderPlan was built by travellers who got lost, ran out of fuel, couldn't find a mechanic at 4,000m, and came back to make sure no one else had to. Every route, every verified stay, every petrol pump — added by people who were actually there.

🤖
AI That Actually Knows India

Our AI isn't a generic trip builder. It's trained on real Ladakh routes, knows that the only pump between Diskit and Pangong is at Tangtse, and understands what ILP permits you need before you even ask.

🤝
Verified by the Community

Every listing — mechanic, homestay, petrol pump, hospital — is submitted and verified by travellers who've been there. Not scraped from the internet. Not guessed. Confirmed by someone who made the call.

🛡️
Safety Is the Feature

Share your live location with family. One-tap emergency contacts. Verified mechanics on every major route. We're building the safety layer that Indian adventure travel has always needed but never had.

Been There.

The team behind WanderPlan has driven the Leh-Manali highway at 4am, gotten stuck on the Spiti Valley road in a hailstorm, searched desperately for a Royal Enfield mechanic between Kaza and Tabo, and had family members panic because there was no signal and no way to check in.
